Safe and Legal Practice

◷ 1 min read⌘ Phone, tablet and PC
Device supportBrowser lessons work on phone and PC. Practical commands include Windows, Linux and Termux routes.Choose setup →

Cybersecurity is learned through practice, but practice must be controlled, lawful and explicitly authorized.

Appropriate environments

  • Devices and accounts you own.
  • Virtual machines created for training.
  • The local challenges included with this project.
  • Purpose-built CTF and vulnerable-lab platforms.
  • Systems covered by clear written or explicit authorization.

Out of scope

Do not scan, exploit, intercept, guess passwords for or disrupt third-party websites, public IP addresses, Wi-Fi networks, accounts or devices without permission. Public accessibility is not authorization.

Lab safety

  1. Use a maintained hypervisor or container platform.
  2. Take snapshots before significant experiments.
  3. Prefer NAT or host-only networking for vulnerable machines.
  4. Keep real credentials, personal information and API keys out of labs.
  5. Review scripts and binaries before execution.
  6. Avoid unrestricted passwordless sudo outside disposable environments.

The original CLI installer and some compiled Linux challenges modify the environment. Run them in Docker or a disposable Linux VM rather than on a primary system.

The objective is learning and defense, never unauthorized access.
