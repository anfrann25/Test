126-Hour Practical Track

A complete local cybersecurity curriculum for Windows, Linux and Android with Termux.

126 hours43 labs3 device paths
How the 126 hours are counted

Time is an estimate for active work: preparation, execution, validation, correction and documentation. Passive reading is not counted. Experienced learners may finish faster; beginners may need longer.
