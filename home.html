THE LEARNING PATH

From first principles to a complete investigation.

Complete each stage in order. Focused lessons prepare you, then practical work makes you use what you learned.

  1. 1
    FOUNDATIONSStart safely

    Understand the field, choose your device and create a safe workspace.

  2. 2
    TERMINALControl your system

    Navigate, inspect files and understand processes and permissions.

  3. 3
    NETWORKSUnderstand communication

    Learn IP, DNS, ports, HTTP and normal network behaviour.

  4. 4
    PYTHONAutomate inspections

    Write small tools that inspect files, logs and changes.

  5. 5
    WEBSecure web applications

    Understand requests, validation, sessions and secure code.

  6. 6
    DEFENCEDefend systems

    Harden devices, monitor activity and triage alerts.

  7. 7
    CAPSTONEInvestigate and report

    Build a timeline, recover safely, verify fixes and complete the capstone.
